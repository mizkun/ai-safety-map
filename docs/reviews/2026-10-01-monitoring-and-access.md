### Evidence colors

The new work does not justify changing any existing level. In particular, C3b's recorded delay is not evidence that harm grew during that delay, let alone that intervention is generally too slow. W2 is still an indirect extrapolation to economy-wide replacement. A detection proof of concept does not show that M3 has been prevented. Conversely, successful shutdown after a local incident does not erase the fact that unauthorized action occurred.
