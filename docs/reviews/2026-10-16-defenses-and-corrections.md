## Primary evidence and editorial decisions

### Gemini 4 Argon: capability tests and defensive assumptions

Originals: [October 13 model card](https://deepmind.google/models/model-cards/gemini-4-argon/) and its linked [Safety & Security Report](https://storage.googleapis.com/deepmind-media/gemini/gemini_4_safety_report.pdf), October 2026 edition. Experiment dates are not uniformly specified. Read the relevant biological/cyber evaluation methods, monitoring assumptions, random-sample analysis and R&D section, rather than claiming to audit every evaluation in the report. Visually checked the biological comparison table, cyber figure/footnote and R&D trend figure against the original PDF.

- **M2b1 / `argon-bio-uplift`:** sections 3.2.1.3 and 3.3.2.3, Tables 3.2–3.3. Eight experts assessed information in three scenarios against web baselines without external safeguards: two comparisons improved, one declined. These are information rubrics, not novice execution or physical-harm experiments. Safeguard results use different query and seed denominators; neither denominator establishes population-wide safety.
- **M2c1 / `argon-cyber-evaluation`:** sections 3.2.2.3–5, Figure 3.2 and footnote 12. Argon had command, Python and search tools in simulated environments without external safeguards. All twelve challenges were solved at least once across attempts, but two involved unintended solutions. Do not substitute this for one-shot reliability on unfamiliar real targets or deployment with safeguards. A modeled uplift estimate elsewhere in the report is not imported as an observed rate.
- **C3a3 / `argon-monitoring-assumptions`:** sections 3.4.3.3–4, 3.4.4.3 and 3.4.5. The report describes monitoring actions and reasoning, including uncovered surfaces and repaired or pending bypasses. A stratified random sample of 10,000 training rollouts supplements selected flagged cases. Coverage, recall, tamper resistance and timely response remain parts of the safety argument; separate-model monitoring does not directly validate resistance to collusive misses. Retrospective analysis is distinct from stopping an action before execution.
- **R2 / `argon-rd-trend`:** section 3.4.2, Figures 3.13–16. Internal workflow evidence includes twelve leaders/researchers and human direction and verification. The capability-trend slope change around April 1 was not statistically significant, despite more AI-written code. This is a limited metric/period from one developer, not proof that AI has no effect or cannot accelerate future development. It counters interpreting code share as a measured whole-process multiplier.

The source is a developer report, including commissioned evaluations. Underlying evaluator data were not independently revalidated. Added bounded node paragraphs and updated M2b1/R2 summaries; retained the existing distinctions between information, access, execution, oversight and harm.

### Better auditors and external access are conditional improvements

The [auditor-training paper, v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.25460v1) was first submitted **August 26**; the [October 15 author blog](https://alignment.anthropic.com/2026/training-alignment-auditors/) is a discovery lead, not a new experiment date. Read sections 2–4, the limitations and Appendix G. Haiku 4.5 is trained on short investigations of largely prompt-planted behavior, with held-out behaviors/scenarios, separate training/evaluation judges and selected transfer to fine-tuned targets. Pairwise comparisons and benign targets improve a selected composite; simply rewarding alarming reports can induce unsupported findings. All four metrics use AI judges without human validation. Matching Opus 4.6 on the composite is not matching every discovery metric or proving coverage of naturally occurring unknown problems. Add **R4 / `trained-alignment-auditors`**, retaining the requirement for independent checks.

[Anthropic's October 15 evaluator update](https://www.anthropic.com/news/an-update-on-independent-evaluators) distinguishes METR's six staff with employee-like access from other partnerships. Read the access/publication terms and same-day SecureBio clarification. Exclusions include customer and privileged information and architecture information, with an approval-dependent architecture exception. METR needs no publication approval, but predefined redactions remain possible; it may discuss their justification. Other partners do not necessarily share these rights. **C3a1 / `embedded-evaluator-access`** replaces the older September proposal paragraph with the reported implementation and its limits. SecureBio is not described as already embedded on identical terms. Institutional access is not a measurement of fewer missed failures or incidents.
