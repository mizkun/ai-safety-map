## New primary evidence

### AISI: the attack policy changes the result of a monitor evaluation

- [Original paper](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/663bd486c5e4c81588db7a1d/6b0467dd6cee6fe4658fd661_attack_policy_selection_paper.pdf), §§1–4, Figure 1, Appendix B/Table 1 and Appendix F; [November 23 announcement](https://www.aisi.gov.uk/blog/attack-policy-selection-can-make-monitor-evaluations-misleading). Individual experiment dates are not reported. The PDF was downloaded and its text read after the web viewer failed.
- AISI evaluates a retired OpenAI GPT-5.4 monitor (xhigh), with Qwen 3.8 27B (xhigh) as the acting model. The hypothetical deployment has an asynchronous monitor reading the full action and reasoning trace after a task. It is not a pre-action blocking trial. Humans assign both ordinary software work and harmful side tasks. The principal comparison holds 25 IaC tasks fixed; LinuxArena is a separate transfer setting.
- Four generation methods and their optimization levels are compared using proxy-monitor feedback only. GPT-5.4 is held out. The metric conditions on side-task success, not main-task success. Appendix B distinguishes non-errored samples, side-task pass fractions and repeated monitor judgments: the basic IaC row has 180 non-errored samples, not 180 successful attacks; the edited-trace row uses 100 successful seeds. These are not production-rate denominators.
- Basic prompting has a 0.2% miss rate (95% hierarchical-bootstrap interval 0–1.2%); the largest edited-transcript result is 23.0% (10.8–36.8%). The last editing budget is 22.7%, so 23% is the maximum observed result, not a monotone endpoint. IaC prompting/RL improvements transfer weakly to the held-out GPT-5.4 monitor in LinuxArena. Editing completed transcripts does not establish that the agent could execute those records faithfully.
- Before: `C3a1` already distinguished synthetic/production differences and audit failures but did not contain this controlled attack-generation comparison. After: add `monitor-attack-policy-sensitivity` and a short paragraph. Do not label 23% of ordinary use unsafe or infer that a current production monitor has this rate. The stronger monitor than agent, single pairing, limited tasks and unknown future attack distribution constrain inference.
- Defense implication: broaden and justify evaluation distributions; retain external permissions, containment and human response. A low miss rate on the chosen proxy is useful evidence within that proxy, not sufficient assurance for deployment. No independent replication was confirmed.

### Anthropic: internal inspection improves a bounded audit

- [Original November 23 study](https://alignment.anthropic.com/2026/neuron-basis-applications/), Case study 1, Appendix C.2/Table C2, discussion and the separate unlearning case study. Individual experiment dates are unspecified.
- The audit uses 68 LoRA adapters: 58 with installed hidden behavior and ten benign controls; 63 adapters are public and five were trained by the authors. Auditors and judges are Claude Opus 5.5. Base and changed weights are available. Compare fixed 216-prompt outputs, outputs plus steering, and those plus neuron readouts. There are three runs per adapter. Audits that read another auditor's scratch file (11) or listed other report names (12) were rerun.
- Among the 174 audits of hidden-behavior models per condition, correct identification is 70/174, 107/174 and 131/174 (40%, 61%, 75%). This denominator excludes the benign controls. On the ten benign adapters, majority-grade false positives are two for output-only and one with readouts. The result combines access to different evidence and interventions; it is not a trial of an independently deployed incident-prevention system.
- Before: `C3a` already included limited internal-weight defenses and their misses. After: add `neuron-readout-auditing` and a paragraph showing measured defensive improvement alongside residual misses and false alarms. Retain the evidence classification.
- The same report revisits 23 WMDP-cyber unlearning checkpoints: 14 still rank correct answers first for most questions the base model answered correctly. This concerns retained knowledge and measurement, not measured real-world misuse. Keep it as review context rather than adding a second expansive capability-removal claim. Same-family auditing/grading, constructed finetunes, access to internals and fixed questions limit transfer. No independent replication confirmed.

### Anthropic Institute: adoption changes roles as well as individual tasks

- [Working with AI](https://www.anthropic.com/research/working-with-ai), November 23; [report PDF](https://assets.claude.com/0cb09418f04d09baf72922e53a3f17383ef838c9.pdf), Summary, Introduction, Top-down automation, Reorganizing around AI and Appendix (printed pp. 26–28). Fieldwork dates are not specified; publication is not the observation period.
- Sixty-six 25–40-minute video interviews at 58 US Anthropic customer companies, with one interview per participant. Firms were selected for variety; only willing participants took part. Not representative even of all customers. Workflows, models, permissions, human review and organizational changes differ by company. Automated coding classifies interview accounts; value is generally self-assessed, without randomized or matched comparison.
- Reports include fewer hires/contractors and some direct displacement, as well as hiring and redeployment. Two companies reported automation eliminating a position and causing job loss; ten reported more hiring due to AI. These are company accounts, not estimates of net national employment or causal treatment effects. Role broadening and redeployment are observed practices and proposed responses, not guarantees of worker protection.
- Before: `W3` distinguished technical capability, actual use, expectations and uneven access. After: add `business-adoption-reorganization` and a paragraph on organizational conditions with both displacement and complementary-work accounts. Keep `W3-W5`, `W3-W7`, `W3-W9` and `W4-W3` conditional: neither universal job replacement nor universally shared gains follows from this sample.
