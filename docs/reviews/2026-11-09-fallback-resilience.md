## Added evidence and editorial decisions

### Skill practice and organizational fallback are different

[Designing Against Deskilling, v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.20143v1), published September 17; experiment in August 2026. Read methods, Table 3, results and limitations. The preregistered trial randomized 704 UK adults across five conditions, using GPT-OSS-120B for fraction practice with answers available on request. Humans solved the final six items without AI. Feedback favored immediate performance under the preregistered one-sided test; its two-sided 95% interval includes no effect. AI access alone did not significantly impair performance, and effort points did not clearly improve it. There is no delayed assessment or organizational handover test.

Before: D2 and D1-D2 described weakening fallback capacity with only broad adoption and disempowerment sources. After: add a concrete, tentative intervention and its limits. This challenges inevitability, not the conditional mechanism. Keep D2's classification and D2-D3/E1 boundaries. Source/card ID: `metacognitive-feedback`. No claim that this prevents durable societal dependence.

### Historical food resilience is not a catastrophe guarantee

[Decoupling food insecurity from shocks](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13598213/), online September 15, September 22 issue. Read Tables 1–2, Figure 2, Discussion and Materials and Methods. The regression sample is 87 countries over 2011–2019; the descriptive figure uses 105. Hybrid models distinguish within-country changes from between-country differences. Redundancy and institutional capacity are associated with undernourishment fluctuations. Acute-shock proxies, omitted variables, endogenous relationships and sample coverage limit inference. No AI model, deployment permissions or autonomous physical actions are tested; institutions' actual responses are not directly measured.

Before: E0/F6 and H-E0/F5-F6 mainly cited scenario and payment-policy material. After: add an empirical preparedness lens while explicitly withholding causal-intervention and worldwide-recovery claims. Source/card ID: `food-system-resilience`. Keep the H/T/X distinction and all classifications. This is not evidence that a particular AI disaster is survivable.

Targeted correction and replication searches for both papers did not establish an independent replication or verified correction. Search absence is not proof that none exists. No model calls, statistical reproduction or raw-data audit was performed.
