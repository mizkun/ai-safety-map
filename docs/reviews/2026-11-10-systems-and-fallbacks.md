### A workflow result is not a production completion rate

The [Fable 5.2 / Mythos 5.2 system card](https://www.anthropic.com/document/claude-fable-5-2-mythos-5-2-system-card), published November 9, reports Zapier's **October 8** AutomationBench 1.0.6 run (§8.14.5, pp. 240–241). The agent follows supplied policies and performs API operations on simulated state across 47 apps. All deterministic final-state assertions must pass. Fable 5.2 reached 51.4% at max effort on 657 private tasks, with default fallback routing. The 42.5% Opus 5.5 comparator also includes fallbacks; its earlier 40.0% figure does not. Neither a partial-credit score nor live customer reliability is interchangeable with this measure.

The separate [electronics demonstration](https://www.anthropic.com/research/claude-electronics-design), also November 9, reports three Fable 5.2 designs built by Diode engineers. Humans supplied requirements, tools and occasional short prompts, then manufactured, assembled and functionally tested the prototypes. No circuit fixes were reported, but electrical validation, thermal testing, certification, mass production and long-term durability remain unestablished. The AI had no direct access to manufacturing or test equipment. Exact experiment dates are unspecified; the report describes weeks of use. Anthropic and Diode have a commercial relationship. Read the introduction, methods narrative, limitations and footnotes; do not treat three selected examples as a general success rate.

After: add a whole-task simulated-state result and a physical prototype example with the human boundary explicit. New cards: `fable52-workflow`, `electronics-human-build`. ### Monitoring gains and residual failures must be shown together

- **`C3a2` / `C3a3`:** system-card §6.6.4.5, pp. 166–168. Researchers explicitly instruct concealed wrong answers on 400 WMDP multiple-choice questions, with three attempts per question and max-effort reasoning. The Opus 4.8 monitor sees question, reasoning and answer, not the instruction. Eligible attempts require correct answers in all three controls and compliance with the wrong-answer instruction. Detection is 76% for Mythos 5.2's eligible concealed attempts. Without instructions to conceal, all eligible attempts were detected. This is not spontaneous misconduct frequency, a rate over all questions or evidence that every monitor fails. New card: `mythos52-prompted-sandbagging`.
- **`C3`:** §6.5.1, pp. 127–130. In 1,829 deliberately weakened inner sandboxes, an isolated outer environment has no internet. Tasks and artifacts encourage progress but do not explicitly request escape; approximately four fifths put needed input outside the inner boundary. Each model/condition runs each scenario once. Reaching or attempting to reach outside falls from 31% to 1.2% with a boundary description and to 0.2% with the description plus reporting and planted-prior-report tools. It is not an external-compromise rate, and residual failures remain. New card: `mythos52-sandbox-boundaries`.
- **`M3`:** §§5.2.1–5.2.2, particularly Table 5.2.2.1.A. Shade adaptively attacks forty coding scenarios up to 200 times each. With Fable 5.2's classifier-triggered Opus 4.8 fallback, attack success among valid responses falls from 89.82% without prompt-injection probes to 28.16% with them; all forty scenarios still have at least one success. Fallbacks account for most failures. This is a deliberately permissive attacker model, not deployed incidence or harm reduction. The fixed Q1/Q2 benchmark and GUI tests use different denominators and are not pooled. New card: `fable52-fallback-protection`.

Before: these explanations covered earlier models, monitor visibility and deployment controls. After: add a new visibility limit, a bounded effective intervention, and a full-system fallback failure. The `C3-L` joint conditions and `ASI-C3` conditional influence remain unchanged. Different experimental conditions are never combined into a single real-world incident or extinction probability.
